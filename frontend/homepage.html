About Hostel

Our student hostel provides the perfect blend of affordable living, academic convenience, and a vibrant community. Designed specifically for university students, we offer comfortably furnished shared and private rooms, dedicated quiet study lounges, and fully equipped communal spaces where you can cook, relax, and connect with peers. Located just minutes from campus, we ensure a safe and secure environment with 24/7 security, high-speed Wi-Fi, and a supportive atmosphere that helps you succeed academically while making lifelong friends.

Features

  • Accommodation: Provides rooms and living spaces for students.
  • Food and Dining: Offers daily meals, drinking water, and dining facilities.
  • Washroom Facilities: Provides shared or attached bathrooms and toilets.
  • Laundry Facilities: Provides washing machines, washing areas, and drying spaces.
  • Study Facilities: Provides quiet study rooms, reading areas, and common study spaces.
  • Recreational Facilities: Includes common rooms, television, indoor games, and outdoor sports facilities.
  • Internet Connectivity: Provides Wi-Fi access for academic and personal use.
  • Security: Includes security personnel, CCTV surveillance, controlled entry, and exit facilities.
  • Water and Electricity: Ensures access to clean water, electricity, and backup power where available.
  • Housekeeping and Cleanliness: Maintains the cleanliness of rooms, corridors, washrooms, and common areas.
  • Medical Facilities: Provides first-aid facilities and access to medical assistance during emergencies.
  • Maintenance Facilities: Supports repairs and maintenance of electrical equipment, plumbing, furniture, and other hostel facilities.
  • Parking Facilities: Provides designated parking spaces for bicycles, motorcycles, and other permitted vehicles.
  • Visitors' Facilities: Provides designated visitor areas and facilities for meeting guests.
  • Common Areas: Includes lounges, seating areas, gardens, and spaces for social interaction.
  • Waste Management: Provides waste collection bins, disposal facilities, and recycling arrangements.
  • Fire Safety: Includes fire extinguishers, emergency exits, alarms, and evacuation arrangements.
  • Accessibility: Provides accessible pathways and facilities for students with disabilities, where available.
