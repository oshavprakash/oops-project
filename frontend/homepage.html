About HostelConnect

HostelConnect is a web-based application designed to simplify and digitize college hostel management. It provides a common platform for students, wardens, and administrators to access and manage hostel-related services efficiently. By reducing paperwork and manual processes, HostelConnect helps improve communication, transparency, and organization while providing students with a convenient way to manage their hostel activities.

Features

  • Hostel Registration & Room Allocation – Apply for hostel accommodation, submit details and documents, and view room allocation.
  • Fee Management – Manage hostel fees, make online payments, and access digital receipts and payment history.
  • Complaint Management – Submit maintenance or disciplinary complaints and track their status.
  • Mess Management – View daily and weekly menus and specify food preferences or dietary requirements.
  • Lost & Found – Report lost belongings or post details of found items to help recover them.
  • Events & Announcements – Stay updated with hostel meetings, celebrations, programs, announcements, and maintenance schedules.
  • Inventory Management – Monitor hostel assets, their condition, maintenance requirements, and replacement records.
  • Roommate Preferences – Specify preferences such as study habits, sleeping schedules, cleanliness, and department for suitable roommate matching.
  • Secure Role-Based Access – Provides separate access and dashboards for students, wardens, and administrators.
