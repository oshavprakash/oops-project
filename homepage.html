About HostelConnect

HostelConnect is a smart hostel services platform developed for **Model Engineering College, Thrikkakara, Kochi, Kerala**. It is designed to simplify hostel-related activities for students and staff by bringing essential services onto a single digital platform. The system helps manage room allocation, fee payments, complaints, mess services, events, inventory, and Lost & Found, providing a more organized and convenient hostel experience.

Features

  • Room Allocation: Streamlined process for assigning rooms to students.
  • Fee Payments: Easy and secure online payment of hostel fees.
  • Complaints: Submit and track complaints related to hostel facilities.
  • Mess Updates: Stay informed about mess menus, timings, and announcements.
  • Events: Get updates on hostel events and activities.
  • Inventory Management: Keep track of hostel inventory and resources.
  • Lost & Found: Report and locate lost items within the hostel.
